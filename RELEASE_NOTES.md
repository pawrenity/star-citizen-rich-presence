## Location artwork refresh

- Replaced 24 location images with different shots from the Star Citizen Wiki, including Lorville, Area18, New Babbage, Levski, and Checkmate.
- Added Wiki planet views for ArcCorp and microTech. Stanton locations that inherit artwork now use the corresponding Wiki image.
- The app uses one image per covered location and keeps the default icon where no suitable image is available. Custom artwork overrides still take precedence.

Download the Windows ZIP under **Assets**, extract it, and launch the executable. Quit the previous companion before upgrading. Desktop preferences carry over.

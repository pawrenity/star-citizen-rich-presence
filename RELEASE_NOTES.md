## 3.1.1: new control panel and in-app updates

- **Redesigned control panel:** a calm dark layout with a sidebar (Card, Activity, Images, Buttons, Updates) and a live preview of your Discord card, including your ship art, contract badge, and play time.
- **Every option in the panel:** Playing, Listening to, Watching, or Competing in; what the member list shows; party status; wiki artwork; and custom images. Before, most of these were only in `scrp.toml`.
- **Custom images:** pick any ship or contract type on the **Images** page, click a wiki picture, click **Upload image** to upload a PNG/JPEG/WebP from your PC (resized to ≤512×512 PNG with metadata stripped and hosted on `catbox.moe` as a public link for Discord), or paste your own `https://` link.
- **In-app updates:** a banner appears when a new version is out. **Update now** downloads the installer, checks it against the release checksums, installs it, and reopens the app. Turn it off under **Check for updates automatically**.
- Modern Windows controls (dark dropdowns and placeholder text).

Privacy: the control panel asks GitHub for the latest version at start and daily, and downloads preview pictures from the Star Citizen Wiki. If you use **Upload image**, the chosen image is resized, stripped of metadata, and uploaded publicly to `catbox.moe` so Discord can show it. See the README.

Download the installer or the Windows ZIP under **Assets**. From 3.1.1 on, installed copies can update themselves.
